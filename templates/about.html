🌐 Mission & Architecture

About FreshChain

Preserving agricultural value and preventing supply-chain food waste through predictive IoT intelligence.

🍃 The Cold Chain Challenge

Post-harvest loss in fruits and perishables exceeds 30% globally due to temperature abuse, inadequate humidity regulation, and poor ventilation during transport. FreshChain combines multi-spectral IoT sensor telemetry (Temperature, Humidity, Light Exposure, and CO₂ gas emissions) with trained machine learning models to detect spoilage onset before visible degradation appears.

⚙️ Machine Learning Pipeline

1. Data Ingestion (M1-M3)

Cleaning 10,900+ raw sensor entries, eliminating duplicates, LabelEncoding fruit species, and standardizing features with StandardScaler.

2. Benchmark Models (M4-M9)

Training Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, Naive Bayes, K-Nearest Neighbors, and Support Vector Machines.

3. Ensembles & Tuning (M10-M12)

Ensemble Hard/Soft voting, stacking classifiers, and hyperparameter tuning leading to our 99.88% accuracy production deployment.
